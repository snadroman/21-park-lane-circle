\texttt{remarks} & \texttt{cond\_exp}, \texttt{cond\_class} (1--5 condition: phrase dictionary + latent-class model, TF-IDF fallback); \texttt{mot\_any}, \texttt{mot\_offers}, \texttt{mot\_quick\_close}, \texttt{mot\_flexible\_close} & Off-market: \texttt{mot\_any} only; list-aware: all four flags \\

\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\small
\caption{Columns dropped}
\label{tab:columns-dropped}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{p{4.2cm}X}
\hline
Column & Reason \\
\hline
\texttt{external\_avm\_estimate}, \texttt{external\_avm\_estimate\_date} & Known only after the sale and absent from the test files; the AVM is dated after the sale, so it would leak the answer. Used only in a noise-floor analysis. \\
\texttt{days\_on\_market} & Known only after the sale. The listing-to-sale gap is taken in months from the two dates instead, on training sales only. \\
\texttt{municipality} & Assigned to communities at random by the generator: explains 4.4\% of community price levels ($p = 0.18$) and 2.3\% of sold/list ($p = 0.52$), about chance. \\
Raw \texttt{latitude} / \texttt{longitude} & Replaced by the 300\,m features: 9.68\% MAPE without raw coordinates vs 9.65\% with them (summer backtest). Raw coordinates mostly reflect random community centres. \\
\hline
\end{tabularx}

\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\small
\caption{Engineered features built and later dropped}
\label{tab:features-dropped}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{p{4.2cm}X}
\hline
Feature & Reason \\
\hline
\texttt{moy}, \texttt{win\_lead} & Month of year; months from listing to the start of the possible sale months. Redundant once targets were measured against the sale month and predictions averaged over possible sale months. \\
\texttt{nn\_level}, \texttt{nn\_dist} & Level of, and distance to, the 10 nearest sales. Replaced by the 300\,m features; \texttt{nn\_dist} added nothing on its own. \\
$\sim$90 other remark tags & Features, target buyer, nearby amenities, writing style. Made the model slightly worse: off-market 10.91\% $\to$ 10.75\% MAPE without them, list-aware 6.09\% $\to$ 6.03\%. \\
Per-component condition & Kitchen, bath, etc.\ scored separately; a single 5-level score did better. \\
Soft pitches in the condition model & Blurred ``dated'' with ``original but in good shape''; the model uses only specific condition phrases. \\
Semantic sentence matching (MiniLM) & 88\% exact condition level on reworded remarks vs 89\% for the final method. \\
Property-type-level trend & No improvement in the Oct 31 experiments. \\
\hline
\end{tabularx}
